## What is an employer branding video?

An employer branding video is a short piece of content that shows candidates what it is actually like to work at your organisation. It communicates your culture, values, and employee experience in a format that text and images simply cannot match.

Unlike corporate promotional videos that focus on products or services, employer branding videos focus on people. They answer the question every candidate asks: "Would I fit in here? Would I be happy?"

The most effective format depends on your audience. Graduate schemes work well with day-in-the-life content showing real work activities. Senior roles benefit from leadership messages that articulate company direction. But across all levels, [employee testimonials consistently outperform](https://www.vouchfor.com/blog/employer-brand-video-benefits) other formats, with 82% of top employer branding efforts featuring them.

### Employee testimonials

The most effective format. Real employees sharing genuine experiences builds trust faster than any scripted message. [Bank of Ireland's employer branding series](https://www.irishjobs.ie/recruiters/recruiters-news/employer-branding-video-series-bank-of-ireland/) focuses on diversity and inclusion stories from actual team members, contributing to their ranking as the 9th most attractive employer among Irish business students.

The key is avoiding over-production. Candidates can spot scripted content immediately, and it undermines trust. Let employees speak in their own words, even if it is not perfectly polished.

### Day-in-the-life

Follow an employee through their actual workday. This format works particularly well for roles where candidates cannot easily picture the day-to-day reality. Chipotle's "How I Chipotle" series interviews people at different levels throughout the business, adding a personal touch by having each interviewee share their order.

### Culture showcases

Office tours, team events, and behind-the-scenes footage. The BBC's "This is Your BBC" campaign used inclusive representation to shift perceptions about respect and opportunity, exceeding campaign targets by 30%.

## How do you create an employer branding video?

Creating effective employer branding videos requires thoughtful planning and, ideally, professional expertise. While the barriers to entry have lowered with smartphone technology, the organisations seeing the strongest results typically invest in professional production or work with multimedia specialists who understand both the technical and strategic elements.

### Step 1: Define your objective

Start with a clear goal. Are you trying to attract graduates? Fill hard-to-hire technical roles? Reduce offer rejections? Your objective shapes everything that follows.

Link your video objectives to your broader [employer value proposition](/blog/employer-branding-ireland-guide). If your EVP highlights career growth, your video should feature employees discussing their development journey. If flexibility is a key differentiator, show what flexible working actually looks like.

### Step 2: Identify the right speakers

The employees who appear in your videos matter more than production quality. Look for people who are genuinely enthusiastic about their work and can articulate why. Avoid the temptation to only feature senior leaders or long-tenured staff.

Diversity matters here too. Candidates want to see people who look like them and came from similar backgrounds. If your videos only feature one type of person, you are narrowing your candidate pool before they even apply.

### Step 3: Prepare guiding questions, not scripts

Give employees guiding questions rather than scripts. "What surprised you most when you joined?" works better than asking them to recite company values. The goal is natural conversation that reveals genuine insights.

Questions that work well:
- What does a typical day look like for you?
- What would you tell someone considering applying here?
- What has been your proudest moment in this role?
- How has your career developed since joining?

### Step 4: Capture the content

The production quality of your video reflects on your employer brand. While smartphones have improved dramatically, professional equipment and expertise make a noticeable difference in lighting, audio clarity, and overall polish.

If working with a professional production team:
- They will handle technical setup, lighting, and audio
- Multiple camera angles create more engaging final content
- Professional editing elevates the storytelling

If capturing content in-house for supplementary social content:
- Film in landscape orientation for LinkedIn and careers pages
- Use good lighting (professional lighting kits are affordable)
- Invest in quality audio (poor sound is the most common issue with amateur video)
- Film multiple takes to give editors options

### Step 5: Edit and distribute

Keep final videos short. Most effective recruitment videos run 1-3 minutes for careers pages and 60-90 seconds for social media. LinkedIn reports that videos under 30 seconds have the highest engagement rates.

Always add captions. Many people watch videos without sound, especially on mobile. Captions also improve accessibility and SEO.

## What mistakes do Irish employers make with video?

After reviewing dozens of employer branding videos from Irish and UK organisations, certain patterns emerge. Here are the mistakes that undermine otherwise good intentions:

<Callout title="Common Mistakes to Avoid" type="warning">
**Too corporate**: Scripted speeches from executives that sound like press releases. Candidates want to hear from people doing the actual work.

**Too polished**: Over-produced videos that feel like advertisements. They trigger scepticism rather than trust.

**Wrong platform format**: A 5-minute video might work on your careers page but will fail on LinkedIn where attention spans are measured in seconds.

**No employee voices**: Videos featuring only b-roll footage of offices and equipment, with voiceover describing culture. Show real people.

**Copying US style**: American employer branding often features enthusiasm levels that feel performative in Irish and UK contexts. Adapt to local norms.
</Callout>

The underlying issue is usually the same: treating employer branding video as a marketing exercise rather than an authentic window into the employee experience. Candidates are sophisticated. They know the difference between genuine insight and corporate messaging.

## How do you get employees to participate?

This is often the hardest part. Many employees are reluctant to appear on camera, worried about how they will come across or concerned about being seen as self-promotional.

Strategies that work:

**Personal asks from managers**: A direct request from someone's manager is far more effective than a company-wide email asking for volunteers.

**Show examples first**: Let employees see videos from colleagues before asking them to participate. This reduces uncertainty about what is expected.

**Promise editorial control**: Assure employees they can review and approve their footage before publication. This dramatically reduces camera anxiety.

**Make it easy**: Schedule filming during work hours in a comfortable location. Remove any friction from participation.

**Employee advocacy programmes**: Formalise the process. Employees who regularly share company content become natural candidates for video participation.

Consider that personal profiles generate [561% more reach](https://dsmn8.com/blog/employer-branding-statistics/) than company pages on LinkedIn. When employees share their own videos, the impact multiplies.

### LinkedIn

The dominant platform for professional content in both Ireland and the UK. With 81.8% adult reach in the UK and similar penetration in Ireland, LinkedIn should be your primary distribution channel.

Best practices for LinkedIn:
- Native upload (not YouTube links) performs better
- Videos under 30 seconds get highest engagement
- Post during business hours (Tuesday-Thursday performs best)
- Add captions (auto-play is muted by default)
- Encourage employees to share from their personal profiles

### Careers page

Your careers page is where serious candidates go to research before applying. Embed videos prominently, not buried in subpages. A landing page with video increases conversion rates by over 80%.

### Job boards

IrishJobs.ie, Indeed, and other platforms increasingly support video embeds in job postings. Use this feature. Job ads with video rank higher in search results and generate significantly more engagement.

### Glassdoor

Your Glassdoor profile is often where candidates go to verify what they see elsewhere. Adding video content here reinforces your employer brand message and gives candidates another touchpoint.

### TikTok and Instagram Reels

For younger candidates (under 30), short-form video on these platforms can be highly effective. The style is different: more casual, faster-paced, and often featuring trending formats or sounds. If your target roles include graduates or early-career professionals, experiment here.

## How do you measure employer branding video ROI?

Measuring the return on employer branding video requires tracking both direct and indirect metrics.

**Direct metrics** you can measure immediately:
- Video views and completion rates
- Engagement (likes, comments, shares)
- Click-through to careers page or job application
- Application conversion rate (views to applications)

**Indirect metrics** that show longer-term impact:
- Cost per hire (should decrease with strong employer brand)
- Time to fill roles (should decrease)
- Quality of applicant pool (measure via hiring manager feedback)
- Offer acceptance rate (strong brands see higher acceptance)
- New hire retention at 6 months (should improve)

The challenge with employer branding is attribution. A candidate might see your video on LinkedIn, research you on Glassdoor, and apply three months later through Indeed. Connecting that journey requires consistent tracking.

Start by adding UTM parameters to links in video descriptions and tracking source data on applications. Over time, survey new hires about what influenced their decision to apply.

For Irish employers, benchmark against the [average cost per hire of €6,000+](https://www.cipd.co.uk/knowledge/fundamentals/people/recruitment/survey-reports). If your video investment reduces cost per hire by even 10%, the ROI calculation becomes straightforward.

<Callout title="Start Small, Measure, Iterate" type="tip">
You do not need a perfect measurement framework before starting. Create your first video, track basic metrics, learn what works, and refine from there. Companies that wait for perfect attribution often never start at all.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long should an employer branding video be?

Most effective employer branding videos are 1-3 minutes for careers pages and 30-60 seconds for social media. LinkedIn reports that videos under 30 seconds have the highest engagement rates. The key is keeping it concise and focusing on one key message per video rather than trying to cover everything.

### How much does an employer branding video cost in Ireland?

Costs vary significantly based on approach. Smartphone-based videos with basic editing run €500-€2,000. Professional agency production for a single video typically costs €2,000-€10,000. Full campaign packages with multiple videos and strategy range from €10,000-€50,000+. Many Irish SMEs start with smartphones and upgrade as they see results.

### What type of employer branding video works best?

Employee testimonials consistently outperform other formats, with 82% of top employer branding efforts featuring them. Day-in-the-life videos and culture showcases also perform well. The key differentiator is authenticity: videos featuring real employees speaking genuinely outperform polished corporate productions.

### Where should I post employer branding videos in Ireland?

LinkedIn is the primary platform, used by 78% of recruiters for video distribution. Also post on your careers page, IrishJobs.ie, Indeed, and your Glassdoor profile. For younger candidates, consider TikTok and Instagram Reels with shorter, more casual clips adapted to those platform norms.

### Should I invest in professional video production?

For your core employer branding content, yes. Professional production ensures proper lighting, clear audio, and polished editing that reflects well on your organisation. While smartphones can work for casual behind-the-scenes social content, your primary recruitment videos benefit from working with a multimedia specialist who understands both the technical and strategic elements of employer branding.
